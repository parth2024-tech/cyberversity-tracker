DATA GOVERNANCE & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Version 2.0

1. Architectural Privacy by Design

AetherGuard operates on a local-first and transparent model. All telemetry, heuristic scoring, and autonomous triage processing are executed in-memory or persisted strictly within the host system's local SQLite database. We do not transmit user browsing patterns, queries, or telemetry to third-party tracking networks.

2. Data Ingestion & Public Feeds

The intelligence aggregated by AetherGuard consists exclusively of publicly accessible metadata harvested from open ecosystem sources, including:

  • arXiv preprints and academic research repositories
  • Public GitHub trending repositories and open-source packages
  • Hacker News community technical discussions and public posts
  • Public RSS feeds from AI model vendors and infrastructure labs

No confidential, private, or non-public data is collected or indexed by this platform.

3. Local Storage & Client State

Client preferences (such as audio volume, narration voice selections, dark theme preferences, and pinned article bookmarks) are stored entirely in your browser's local localStorage or session storage. They remain on your device and are never synchronized to external telemetry providers.

4. Optional Integrations (Telegram & SMTP)

If you configure optional delivery channels (e.g., Telegram bot tokens or custom SMTP credentials for automated newspaper PDF dispatch), those credentials are stored strictly in your local server environment (.env) and are utilized solely to dispatch notifications directly to your designated recipient addresses.

5. Data Retention & Hygiene

AetherGuard enforces automated and manual data retention controls. Ingested entries older than 14 days are discarded in accordance with our real-time freshness policy. Permanent retention only applies to items explicitly saved to the SQLite Permanent Vault by the user.

6. Contact & Source Code

AetherGuard is an open system. For verification or inquiries regarding data handling, inspect the source code directly on GitHub or contact the repository administrator.
